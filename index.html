Manufacturing Excellence

Precision Engineering Process

Our ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing facility follows rigorous quality processes from raw material to delivery.

01
Material Sourcing & QC

Premium-grade lithium cells sourced from certified global suppliers with incoming quality control and traceability.

02
Cell Grading & Matching

Automated cell sorting for capacity, IR, and voltage matching using precision testing equipment for optimal pack performance.

03
Pack Assembly

Robotic laser welding, busbar connection, and structural enclosure assembly in a cleanroom environment.

04
BMS Integration & Flashing

Custom BMS firmware calibration, CAN communication setup, and safety threshold configuration for each application.

05
Formation Cycling & Testing

Multi-cycle formation, capacity verification, impedance spectroscopy, and thermal stress testing per AIS-156 standards.

06
Dispatch & Support

Final IP-rated enclosure, documentation package, and real-time tracking with lifetime technical support.

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our EV components and services.

Ask Us Anything
What minimum order quantity do you accept?+
We accept MOQs starting from 10 units for battery packs and 50 units for BMS modules. For prototyping and R&D, we accept single-unit orders. Contact our sales team for custom requirements.
Do you offer custom battery pack design?+
Yes, our engineering team specializes in custom battery pack design. We handle everything from cell selection and pack architecture to BMS firmware and mechanical enclosure design. Typical lead time for custom development is 8–12 weeks.
What warranty do you offer on battery packs?+
Our standard battery packs come with a 2-year or 1000-cycle warranty (whichever is first). Premium packs qualify for extended 3-year warranty programs. Our BMS includes lifetime firmware update support.
Are your products FAME II eligible?+
Yes, our battery packs and EV components meet all FAME II and PLI scheme requirements. We provide complete documentation for subsidy claims and have helped over 50 OEM partners with their FAME II applications.
Do you provide installation and after-sales support?+
Absolutely. We offer on-site installation assistance, integration support, and 24/7 technical helpdesk. Our service network covers 18 states across India with authorized service partners.
