Our Core Research Areas

🤖
AI-Powered Battery Monitoring
Our ML models process real-time cell voltage, temperature, and current data to predict battery health, estimate remaining useful life (RUL), and detect early signs of thermal runaway with 99.7% accuracy.
STATUS: PRODUCTION READY
🔬
Solid-State Battery Research
In partnership with IIT Gandhinagar, we're developing sulfide-based solid electrolyte systems targeting 400+ Wh/kg energy density and 10-minute fast charging capability for next-gen EVs.
STATUS: PHASE II RESEARCH
⚡
Ultra-Fast Charging Protocols
Developing adaptive CC-CV charging algorithms that extend cycle life by 35% while achieving 80% SOC in under 15 minutes for LFP chemistry through precision thermal management.
STATUS: PILOT TESTING
🏭
Smart Manufacturing & Automation
Digital twin simulation of our entire production line, AI-driven quality inspection using computer vision, and predictive maintenance reducing downtime by 60%.
STATUS: PRODUCTION DEPLOYED
♻️
Second-Life Battery Systems
Algorithm for rapid battery health assessment to grade retired EV batteries for second-life stationary storage applications, extending economic value by 8–12 additional years.
STATUS: PILOT DEPLOYMENT
🌡️
Advanced Thermal Management
Phase-change material (PCM) and immersion cooling research for high-power density battery packs targeting sub-5°C cell temperature variation across entire pack during 6C fast charging.
STATUS: LAB VALIDATION
Lab Infrastructure

World-Class Innovation Labs

🔭
Materials Characterization Lab

SEM, TEM, XRD, XPS, BET surface area analysis, and Raman spectroscopy for cell material and degradation studies.

📡
Electrochemical Testing Suite

192-channel Neware cyclers, EIS spectroscopy stations, dV/dQ analysis, and coin/pouch cell prototyping capability.

💻
AI & Simulation Cluster

NVIDIA GPU cluster for ML model training, physics-based battery simulation using COMSOL/MATLAB, and digital twin deployment.

🧪
Solid-State Prototyping Lab

Argon glove boxes, dry room (dew point -60°C), thin-film deposition, and all-solid-state cell assembly capability.

🌡️
Abuse Testing Chamber

UN 38.3 certified nail penetration, crush, overcharge, short-circuit, and thermal abuse testing facility with gas analysis.

🤝
Academic Collaboration Hub

Joint research facility with IIT Gandhinagar, CSIR-CECRI Karaikudi, and TIFR Mumbai for fundamental battery science.

Our Process

From Lab to Line

Our structured research-to-production pipeline ensures every innovation is rigorously validated before deployment.

Ideation to Prototype3–6 months
Prototype to Validation6–12 months
Validation to Pilot12–18 months
Pilot to Mass Production18–24 months
01
Problem Identification

Market feedback, field data, and scientific literature identify performance gaps and customer pain points.

02
Hypothesis & Simulation

Physics-based models and ML simulations guide hypothesis formulation and predict outcomes before lab work begins.

03
Lab Prototyping

Coin and pouch cell prototyping followed by pack-level validation against performance targets in our test chambers.

04
Regulatory Validation

Third-party testing at NABL-accredited labs for AIS, IEC, UL, and CE certification requirements.

05
Pilot Production

50–500 unit pilot runs on our production line with stringent statistical process control and yield analysis.

06
Mass Deployment

Scaled production with automated quality gates, field monitoring, and continuous improvement feedback loops.
