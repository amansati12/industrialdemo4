Battery Recycling

Closing the Battery Loop

Our CPCB-authorized in-house battery recycling facility processes end-of-life battery packs using a proprietary hydrometallurgical process achieving 95% lithium, cobalt, and nickel recovery — far exceeding India's 70% minimum requirement.

95%
Material Recovery
500+
Packs Recycled/Year
  • ♻️ Lithium carbonate recovery for new cells
  • ♻️ Cobalt and nickel sulphate extraction
  • ♻️ Aluminium and copper scrap reclamation
  • ♻️ Black mass sale to cell manufacturers
  • ♻️ Zero hazardous waste to landfill
