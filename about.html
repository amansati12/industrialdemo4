Who We Are

Engineering Excellence at Every Volt

VoltEdge EV Components Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 2013 by a team of IIT engineers and industry veterans who saw the electric vehicle revolution on the horizon. What began as custom battery packs for local e-rickshaw manufacturers has grown into India's most trusted EV component supplier.

Today, our 650+ person team operates a vertically integrated manufacturing campus in GIDC Gandhinagar, Gujarat — covering everything from cell-level engineering to full system integration. We've shipped over 850 battery systems across 18 Indian states and 3 export markets.

Our Journey

12 Years of Innovation

From a startup dream to India's premier EV component company — a decade of relentless growth.

2013
Company Founded
Established in a 2,000 sq.ft. facility in Gandhinagar with a team of 8 engineers, focused on custom 48V battery packs for local e-rickshaws.
2015
First OEM Partnership
Signed our first OEM supply agreement with GreenWheels Motors, Pune. Scaled production to 100 packs/month. ISO 9001 certification achieved.
2017
BMS Division Launch
In-house Battery Management System R&D division established. First proprietary BMS product launched — VoltGuard Series I.
2019
New Manufacturing Campus
Moved to 60,000 sq.ft. Phase II GIDC facility. Launched EV charger product line. Team grew to 200+ employees.
2021
Series B Funding & Expansion
Raised ₹120 Cr Series B funding. Expanded to 1.2 lakh sq.ft. campus. Entered BESS market for solar energy storage.
2023
Export Markets & AI Integration
Commenced exports to Southeast Asia and Africa. Launched AI-powered predictive BMS — VoltGuard Series III. 850+ battery packs shipped milestone.
2025
Next-Gen Lab Opening
Inauguration of 10,000 sq.ft. Advanced Battery Research Lab. Partnership with IIT Gandhinagar for solid-state battery research. 650+ team members.

Manufacturing Infrastructure

World-Class Facilities

🏗️
Cell Testing Lab

192-channel Neware formation systems, EIS spectroscopy, thermal abuse testing chambers, and automated sorting equipment for 100% cell characterization.

🤖
Robotic Assembly Line

6-axis collaborative robots for laser welding, busbar stacking, and module assembly. Capable of 200 battery packs per day at full capacity.

🌡️
Climate Test Chambers

Walk-in thermal cycling chambers (-40°C to +85°C), vibration test rigs, IP67 submersion tanks, and altitude simulation for 100% production testing.

🔬
Advanced Research Lab

10,000 sq.ft. dedicated R&D facility with SEM/XRD analysis, solid-state battery prototyping, AI simulation cluster, and battery lifecycle testing.

⚡
Charger Test Bay

High-power test bays up to 250 kW for charger validation, OCPP protocol testing, EMI/EMC compliance, and AC/DC safety testing per IEC 61851.

♻️
Recycling Unit

In-house battery dismantling and material recovery facility achieving 95% material recovery rate for end-of-life battery packs. CPCB authorized.
